Reka AI released CS2-10k, a dataset featuring 600,000+ player-round videos spanning 10,000+ hours of first-person footage extracted from professional Counter-Strike 2 matches. The resource pairs video with per-frame annotations including keyboard inputs, mouse movement, and player positioning to enable training of interactive world models. An accompanying open-source rendering pipeline lets researchers generate additional datasets from CS2 demo files.
